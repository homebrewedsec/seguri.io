## BlueTeamCon 2025 - The Premier Conference for Cybersecurity Defenders

**Dates:** September 6-7, 2025 (Training: September 4-5)  
**Location:** Fairmont Chicago Millennium Park  
**Address:** 200 N Columbus Dr, Chicago, IL 60601

---

Join us at **BlueTeamCon 2025**, the only annual in-person conference created specifically for cybersecurity defenders. This unique event brings together 850+ community-minded defensive cybersecurity experts for information sharing, relationship building, and defense-focused presentations.

BlueTeamCon is designed for anyone interested in safeguarding organizations, making it the perfect venue to connect with like-minded professionals and explore the latest defensive strategies and technologies. The conference offers an inclusive environment where defenders can learn, share experiences, and build valuable professional relationships.

### Why Attend BlueTeamCon?

- **Defender-Focused Content**: Sessions specifically designed for blue team professionals
- **Networking Opportunities**: Connect with 850+ defensive security experts
- **Professional Development**: Earn up to 32+ CPE credits over 4 days
- **Training Sessions**: Additional hands-on training available September 4-5
- **Community Building**: Be part of the defensive cybersecurity community

## Connect with Seguri

Don't miss this opportunity to meet our team and discuss how we can support your defensive cybersecurity needs. Whether you're interested in our threat modeling services, incident response planning, or SIEM setup and tuning, we're here to help strengthen your organization's security posture.

We'll be available throughout the conference to discuss tailored solutions for your unique security challenges and explore how Seguri can become your trusted security partner.
