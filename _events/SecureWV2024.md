## SecureWV 2024 - West Virginia's Premier Cybersecurity Conference

**Dates:** March 21-22, 2024  
**Location:** Charleston Coliseum & Convention Center  
**Address:** 200 Civic Center Dr, Charleston, WV 25301

---

**SecureWV 2024** brought together cybersecurity professionals, researchers, and industry leaders for West Virginia's premier cybersecurity conference. This annual event focused on advancing cybersecurity knowledge and building connections within the regional security community.

The conference featured cutting-edge presentations, hands-on workshops, and networking opportunities designed to address the evolving threat landscape facing organizations across West Virginia and the broader Appalachian region.

### Conference Highlights

SecureWV 2024 delivered exceptional value through:

- **Expert Presentations**: Industry-leading speakers sharing practical insights and real-world experiences
- **Technical Workshops**: Hands-on training sessions covering emerging security technologies and techniques
- **Regional Focus**: Content tailored to the unique cybersecurity challenges facing Appalachian organizations
- **Community Building**: Networking opportunities with local and regional cybersecurity professionals
- **Professional Development**: CPE credits and career advancement opportunities

### What Made SecureWV Special

- **Local Expertise**: Showcasing cybersecurity talent and innovation within West Virginia
- **Industry Collaboration**: Bringing together public and private sector security professionals
- **Practical Focus**: Sessions designed to provide immediately actionable security insights
- **Community Investment**: Supporting the growth of cybersecurity capabilities in the region
- **Accessible Format**: Professional-level content in a welcoming, approachable environment
