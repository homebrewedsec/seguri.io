## RSTCon 2025 - Resetting the Cybersecurity Conference Experience

**Dates:** October 24-26, 2025  
**Location:** Hotel Indigo Savannah Historic District  
**Address:** Near River Street, Savannah, GA

---

Join us at **RSTCon 2025**, a conference designed to reset the cybersecurity conference experience with a focus on critical infrastructure security. RSTCon targets the unique challenges facing transportation, energy, manufacturing, and the sensors, systems, and architectures they depend on.

This open forum provides an opportunity to share, demonstrate, and discuss novel findings in critical infrastructure security. RSTCon brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry professionals to address the evolving threat landscape facing our most essential systems.

### What Makes RSTCon Unique

- **Critical Infrastructure Focus**: Specialized content for transportation, energy, and manufacturing sectors
- **Open Forum Format**: Collaborative environment for sharing novel research and findings
- **Systems-Level Approach**: Discussion of sensors, architectures, and integrated security solutions
- **Industry Collaboration**: Bringing together researchers and practitioners
- **Innovative Content**: Fresh perspectives on infrastructure security challenges

### Call for Papers

RSTCon 2025 is currently accepting paper submissions. If you're working on cutting-edge research or have practical insights into critical infrastructure security, consider submitting your work to be part of this important conversation.
