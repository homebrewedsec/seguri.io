## CarolinaCon 2025 - "Budget Cuts" Edition

**Dates:** November 8-9, 2025  
**Location:** To be announced  
**Address:** Details coming soon

---

Join us at **CarolinaCon 2025**, North Carolina's premier hacker conference, returning for another year of cutting-edge cybersecurity content and community building. This year's theme, "Budget Cuts," promises to explore how security professionals can do more with less while maintaining robust defensive postures.

CarolinaCon has established itself as a key event in the Southeast cybersecurity community, bringing together hackers, security professionals, researchers, and enthusiasts for knowledge sharing and networking in a collaborative environment.

### The "Budget Cuts" Theme

In an era where many organizations are being asked to maintain or improve their security posture with constrained resources, CarolinaCon 2025 will focus on:

- **Resource Optimization**: Maximizing security impact with limited budgets
- **Creative Solutions**: Innovative approaches to common security challenges
- **Open Source Tools**: Leveraging community-driven security solutions
- **Efficiency Strategies**: Streamlining security operations and processes
- **Cost-Effective Defense**: Building robust security programs without breaking the bank

### What to Expect

CarolinaCon consistently delivers high-quality content across a broad spectrum of cybersecurity topics, from technical deep-dives to strategic security discussions. The conference fosters an inclusive environment where both seasoned professionals and newcomers can learn and contribute.
