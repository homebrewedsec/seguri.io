## Secure Your Critical Operations

Your operational technology (OT) environments are the backbone of your critical operations, but they face unique security challenges that traditional IT security solutions simply can't address. Our OT Managed Detection and Response services bridge the gap between IT and OT security, providing specialized monitoring and response capabilities designed specifically for industrial control systems, SCADA networks, and critical infrastructure.

## The OT Security Challenge

Operational technology environments present distinct challenges that require specialized expertise:

### Unique Operational Requirements
- **Always-on availability**: Systems that can't afford downtime
- **Real-time performance**: Microsecond timing requirements
- **Safety-critical operations**: Human and environmental safety implications
- **Legacy systems**: Decades-old equipment with limited security features

### Specialized Threat Landscape
- **Nation-state adversaries**: Advanced persistent threats targeting critical infrastructure
- **Industrial-specific malware**: Threats like Stuxnet, TRITON, and Industroyer
- **Insider threats**: Privileged access to critical systems
- **Supply chain attacks**: Compromised industrial equipment and software

### Compliance and Regulatory Pressure
- **NERC CIP**: Critical Infrastructure Protection standards
- **TSA Directives**: Pipeline and rail security requirements
- **CISA Guidelines**: Critical infrastructure cybersecurity frameworks
- **Industry Standards**: IEC 62443, NIST Cybersecurity Framework
